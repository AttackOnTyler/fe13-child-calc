# Q9: Marriages, Robin, and whether a child is worth recruiting (notebook answer, condensed)

The notebook's citation numbers now run past 500, so the livestreams the user added are being used.

## Robin
- **+Mag / −Str is "objectively" the best.** Str is a dump stat for a magic Robin. +Spd is rejected as overrated, and +Def as a "beginner crutch" that hurts caps later.
- **M!Robin spouse:** **Tharja** is the top pick (Hex/Anathema, Dark Mage Morgan with Anathema and Solidarity, Galeforce and Dark Mage for Noire). Also Nowi (dragon Morgan; Galeforce and Dark Mage for Nah), Lissa (fast S; Owain and Morgan paralogs early), Cordelia (Severa).
- **F!Robin spouse:** **Chrom** (god-tier Lucina and Morgan), but that takes Chrom away from Sumia, so fewer top-tier units come out overall. Frederick or Vaike suit a physical F!Robin.

## Main story marriages without grinding
- M!Robin × Tharja (or Lissa / Nowi)
- **Chrom × Maribelle**: the best Chrom pairing without grinding. Lucina gets magic modifiers, and Brady gets Rightful King, Luna and staves.
- Lon'qu × Miriel (Laurent with Vantage and Dark Mage)
- Libra (or Henry) × Lissa (Owain as a Vantage + Nosferatu tank)
- Stahl × Cordelia (Severa)
- Vaike × Cherche (Gerome)
- Gregor × Panne (Yarne)
- Henry × Sumia (Cynthia: Dark Mage + Galeforce + Dual Support+)

## Pairings he warns against
- **Donnel × anyone** in a run without grinding.
- **Frederick × a combat child** (−Spd, no Faire). It's fine for Yarne.
- **Henry × Miriel:** redundant, since Laurent already has Dark Mage.
- **Tharja × Henry:** overlapping class sets.
- **Ricken × a physical mother:** ruins the Str/Spd modifiers.

## Is a child worth recruiting? (no grinding)
- He weighs three things: **starting class** (fewer seals), **training medium** (staves give free EXP; tomes and bows chip safely) and **paralog timing and difficulty**.
- **Top recruits:** Brady (easiest: staves up to Lv 20), Kjelle (earliest paralog), Morgan, Severa, Cynthia.
- **Skipped or benched:** Yarne (Taguel, reclass resets ranks to E), Lucina (Ch 13 timer, Lv 10 unpromoted, weak without a Robin mother), Nah (slow Manakete, no Galeforce or staves), Laurent (reclassing to Dark Mage loses staves).
