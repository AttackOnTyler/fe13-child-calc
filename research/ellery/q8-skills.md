# Q8: Skill tier list and combos (notebook answer, condensed)

## Main story Lunatic+ (no grinding)
- **S:** Veteran; Hex + Anathema; Dual Support+ + Demoiselle; Sol (physical EP sustain); Dual Guard+ (back row, stops Hawkeye/Luna+/Counter); Vantage; the Breakers (dodge tanking).
- **A:** Dual Strike+; evasion stack (Avoid +10, Quick Burn, Lucky 7, Outdoor Fighter); Faires (+5, apply on dual strikes); Galeforce (S when it's easy to reach, but a "noob trap" if you force Pegasus Lissa or Olivia through E lances); Solidarity, Charm; Str +2 / Mag +2 passed down as a "free tonic".
- **B:** Aether (limited users); Luna (inconsistent, no dual-strike procs); Vengeance (risky without Vantage + Nosferatu); Lifetaker, Renewal; Lethality; rallies (only the ones picked up naturally).
- **C / overrated:** **Armsthrift ("the most overrated skill")**; **Astra ("virtually unusable in Lunatic+", 5 hits into Counter)**; Miracle.
- **F:** Slow Burn; Aptitude.

## Apotheosis / postgame
- **S:** Limit Breaker (#1); Galeforce; Aggressor; Vantage + Vengeance + Wrath (+ Nosferatu); Faires; All Stats +2; rallies, packed onto 2 bots (Robin, Maribelle).
- **A:** Aether, Luna; Dual Support+ / Dual Guard+ / Dual Strike+; Iote's Shield.
- **Low:** Pavise, Aegis, Renewal, Lifetaker; Aptitude; Armsthrift.

## Anti-synergies
- **Sol + Luna:** proc order is Aether > Astra > Sol > Luna > Ignis > Lethality, so Sol pre-empts Luna. (The app already curates this in B12.)
- **Astra + Counter:** in Lunatic+ this kills your own unit.
- **Vengeance + Sol / Nosferatu:** healing lowers Vengeance damage on the next attacks.
- **Procs on backs are wasted:** Luna/Astra/Sol/Aether/Ignis don't fire on dual strikes. Backs want Faires, Hex/Anathema, Dual Support+, Zeal/Gamble.

## Synergies
- Vantage + Vengeance + Wrath + Nosferatu (100% crit EP loop).
- Hex + Anathema.
- Dual Support+ + Demoiselle + Solidarity (aura stack).
- Galeforce + Aggressor + Faire + All Stats +2 / Limit Breaker (PP removal stack).
- Avoid +10 + Quick Burn + Lucky 7 + Breaker (dodge tank).
- Rightful King + Aether/Luna.
- Longbow + Faire + Dual Strike+ from the back row, safe against Counter.
