# Q2: Army composition (notebook answer, paraphrased)

- **Main campaign template, 12 slots** (How to Build a TEAM for Lunatic+, around the Ch. 12 benchmark), expanding to 14–15 late or on DLC maps:
  - 4 leads: 2 enemy-phase sweepers (Nosferatu or Sol/Breaker tanks) and 2 player-phase removers (Galeforce)
  - 4 pair-up backs, one behind each lead
  - 1 dancer (Olivia)
  - 3 staff users / Rescue bots (15 Rescue charges)
- **Rally, main campaign:** no dedicated Gen 1 rally bots, since the extra classes cost EXP. Rallies come with classes already in use, or go to Gen 2 children (**Brady, Morgan**) as low-effort rally bots (2nd Generation EXPLAINED).
- **Rally, postgame / Apotheosis:** every rally is packed onto **2 dedicated Rally Bots**, Robin and Maribelle, both Gen 1 (It's Time To Optimize Lunatic+ [Step 1: Team Building]).
- **Main story vs. Apotheosis:** in the main story Gen 1 does the fighting and Gen 2 is optional or support/rally. In Apotheosis the roles invert: **Gen 2 children are the 4 leads + 4 backs** (higher caps, more skills) and Gen 1 takes the rally bot slots.
- **DLC maps:** about 12–14 units. Galeforce leads with Longbow backs and Rescue staves.
