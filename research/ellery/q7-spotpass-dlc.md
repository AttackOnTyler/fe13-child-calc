# Q7: SpotPass, Einherjar, DLC maps and skills (notebook answer, condensed)

**Flag:** this answer mixes up sources. Priam comes from a paralog, not SpotPass. Which DLC map awards which skill (Lost Bloodlines appears twice, and Paragon is attributed to it) should be checked against `src/game-data` and the disagreements log before anything is curated from it.

## Bonus units
- **Einherjar / DLC heroes** (Marth, Ike, Roy, Lyn, Alm, Micaiah, …): static "Robin clones" with a fixed asset/flaw and locked skills. They are free fillers in the main story and outclassed in the postgame (no S-supports, no inheritance). Micaiah comes with Shadow Gift and Rally Luck; Alm is a Dread Fighter with Aggressor.
- **SpotPass / late units:** useless in the main story because they join at the end.
  - **Aversa:** Shadow Gift, so she can use dark magic in Dark Flier or Sage (niche, top tier).
  - **Emmeryn:** a high-Mag late staff / Rescue bot.
  - **Walhart:** Great Knight with Conquest.
  - **Priam:** Ragnell (15 Mt, 1–2 range, can't be forged).
  - **Gangrel, Yen'fay:** solid pre-promotes with few supports.

## DLC maps (timing, reward)
- Champions of Yore: early to mid game, **All Stats +2**. Beat it once and don't farm it on a run without grinding.
- Lost Bloodlines: mid to late game, **Dread Fighter + Aggressor**, and **Paragon** (listed twice).
- Smash Brethren: mid to late game, **Wedding Bouquet** (Bride) and **Iote's Shield**.
- Rogues & Redeemers: postgame, **Limit Breaker**. This is the most mandatory one.
- Golden Gaffe / EXPonential Growth: postgame gold and EXP farming.
- Child paralogs (Ch 13–18): do them as soon as they unlock, for free map EXP and Rescue charges.

## Skill verdicts
- **Limit Breaker:** must-have #1 for the postgame, on every combat unit.
- **Aggressor:** S tier on player phase (+10 damage when initiating), mandatory for Galeforce attackers.
- **All Stats +2:** S tier, costs little.
- **Iote's Shield:** essential for fliers.
- **Shadow Gift:** dark magic outside Dark Mage classes.
- **Paragon:** a training tool.
- **Rightful King:** +10% proc rate.
- **Conquest:** removes armor and cavalry weaknesses.
