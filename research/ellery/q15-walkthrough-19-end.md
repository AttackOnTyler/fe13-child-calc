## Remaining paralogs
- **P1 Donnel**, after Ch 3–4: recruit him, then bench him (EXP belongs on Sumia/Robin/Vaike).
- **P2–3 Anna / Sumia's paralogs**, after Ch 8: Dark Flier Sumia + Chrom clear both; Physic, Blessed Bow, Master Seal.
- **P16 Nah**, ~Ch 18–21: **wait 10 turns** outside the collapsing walls so the Mire sorcerers burn their charges, then walk in.
- **P20 Emmeryn**, after Ch 25: **T1 Rescue Emmeryn** to the base; she replaces Anna as an S-tier staff user.
