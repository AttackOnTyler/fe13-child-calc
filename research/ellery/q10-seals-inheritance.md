# Q10: Seals, reclassing, inheritance (notebook answer, condensed)

- **Master Seal** when a unit needs stats *now*, hits a class stat cap (Robin: Mag 31 in Tactician → Sorcerer), or needs mobility (Frederick → Wyvern Lord for the Ch 12 desert). Source: When to Master Seal in Lunatic+ [Step 3: Promotions].
- **Promote at Lv 10 or wait for 20?** It depends on benchmarks: keep base-leveling while the unit holds up, and promote at 15–17 once it falls behind. Robin (Veteran) can reach 20.
- **Lv 30 trick:** Taguel, Manakete, Dancer and Villager level to 30, then take one Second Seal straight into an advanced class (Olivia → Dark Flier; Panne, Nowi, Nah → Wyvern Lord or Sage).
- **Reclass traps:** a new base class resets weapon rank to E. Examples: Donnel (Villager → Merc/Fighter), Panne at Lv 10, Nowi → Wyvern Rider, Laurent → Dark Mage.
- **Seals without grinding:** before Ch 12, 2–3 Master Seals and 1–2 Second Seals. Master Seals become buyable after Ch 12, Second Seals after Ch 16. Early seals go to carries; Lissa stays a Cleric through Ch 12.
- **Inheritance:** the child gets each parent's **last equipped skill** (the notebook says "when the paralog is generated/entered"; check this against `research/skill-inheritance`).
- **Bench-father trick:** park Virion at Lv 10 Archer. Right before the child's paralog, Second Seal him to Lv 1 Mage or Wyvern Rider so he learns Mag +2 / Str +2, put that skill last, and the child inherits it.
- **Class sets:** a child gets both parents' base class trees, so 8–10+ promoted classes. Male-only classes convert for daughters (the notebook says Fighter → Pegasus Knight via Gaius, Donnel and M!Robin; check this against `src/game-data/children.ts`). Morgan gets every non-special class.
