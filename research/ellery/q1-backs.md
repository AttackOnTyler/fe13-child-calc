# Q1: How Ellery picks pair-up backs (notebook answer, paraphrased)

- Core structure: **4 leads + 4 support backs** (How to Build a TEAM for Lunatic+).
- Not one stat and not "the weakest". The criteria are:
  1. **Units that fall off as leads or do better as backs.** Frederick (low Res, can't double) becomes a permanent back. Henry is a "pre-packaged" back (Hex/Anathema). Nah: sitting in the back row cancels her poor Spd while her Str/Def/Mag modifiers buff the lead (DLC Showcase).
  2. **Pair-up bonuses tailored to the lead's deficit.** Spd matters most (Trickster, Assassin, Swordmaster, Pegasus backs). Def/Str come from Knight/General (Kellam). Mag/Def/Mov from Dark Knight/Sorcerer.
  3. **Skills that work from the back row.** Hex/Anathema ("meta-defining"), Dual Guard+, Dual Support+/Demoiselle (Valkyrie), Solidarity (Robin/Morgan), Faires (they apply on dual strikes, while Luna/Astra don't).
  4. **Dual-strike utility.** Snipers with a Longbow (Chrom, Noire). **Gerome and Yarne** lack Galeforce and Dark Mage, so they are not S-tier leads but are "top-tier A-rank" backs behind a Galeforce/Anathema lead (2nd Generation EXPLAINED).
